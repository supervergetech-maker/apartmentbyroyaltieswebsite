# Apartments by Royalties — Luxury Hospitality & Real Estate Website

The official, high-performance web platform for **Apartments by Royalties** (Lagos, Nigeria).

## Key Features & Architectural Highlights
- **Authentic Photography & Real Assets**: High-resolution imagery for all verified shortlets, executive car fleet, and yachts.
- **Executive & Luxury Car Fleet**: 2024 Lamborghini Urus, 2024 & 2017 Rolls-Royce, 2025 Range Rover Autobiography, Lexus GX 460, Toyota Hilux Adventure, Mercedes-Benz Viano VIP Bus, and Mercedes-Benz C300.
- **Waterfront Boat & Yacht Charters**: Motor yachts, house boats, party boats, and jet skis, with refundable damage deposit policies.
- **YouTube Video Showcase**: Lazy-loaded video tours.
- **Property Sales & Joint Venture Hub**: Property acquisitions and development partnerships across Lagos.
- **Royal Splash Screen**: Brand logo loading display with a cross-fade animation.
- **Direct WhatsApp Concierge**: Booking links with pre-filled messages across services.
